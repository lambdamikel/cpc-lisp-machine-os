CPC Lisp 0.95 on a real TRS-80 -- what only the hardware can tell
================================================================

Everything else was checked in trs80gp (the CPC's tests, the demos).
Tick what you see; note anything that differs.

The disc: build/trs80/lisp-a.dsk for a Model I (JV1, LDOS 5.3.1, single
density), lisp-m3.dsk for a Model III (JV3, LDOS 5.1.4). X-MEM/80 in its
X-MEM mode (not SuperMem).

1. [ ] Boot it, answer LDOS's date (and time), type LISP. It says
       "loading" and the seven names, then:

           CPC Lisp 0.95
           A Lisp machine for the TRS-80
           library ROMs 3 4 5 6
           2832 cells free

           LISP>

2. [ ] (loop)  then BREAK  : "Error: break", and LISP> again
3. [ ] SHIFT down-arrow, let go, then -  : a ~ appears
4. [ ] (sound 1 284 100)  : one second of A 440 from the cassette
       port (an amplifier on it)
5. [ ] (say "hello, I am a T R S 80")  : Talker/80 speaks it;
       (sing)  : the whole line sung, "I am a Lisp machine"
       (bytes now go 0.5 ms apart: the song stopped after "I am a")
6. [ ] (defun f () 42)  (save "m:x")  (dir "m:")  : X.LSP listed;
       press RESET, LISP again, (dir "m:")  : still there
7. [ ] (shapes)  : discs and boxes in blocks; a key clears it.
       (demo) lists the others, with a pause half way.
       (dir) on a full disc, SHIFT @ held: it stops; a key goes on.
       (defun f (x) (* x x)) (ed 'f)  : the cursor a block about a
       letter's height; the arrows only move it, SHIFT <- deletes to
       the left, CLEAR under it (at LISP> too)
8. [ ] With MIDI/80 (its DIP switches to the Waveblaster or MIDI OUT):
       (midi-tune)  : a minuet, harpsichord and cello, about 25 s
9. [ ] On a Model III: 1, 4 and 6 again (its clock and ROM are
       elsewhere), and  (let ((t0 (time))) (sleep 2) (- (time) t0))
       : about 600
